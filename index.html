How the Pipeline Works

Three specialized AI agents, orchestrated by a manager agent on Lyzr Studio.

1

You enter a target company

Provide the company name and any context you have. More detail means more targeted output from the pipeline.

2

Company Research Agent fires

Powered by Tavily web search. Researches the company's product, funding stage, recent news, AI adoption signals, and assigns an ICP fit score.

3

Contact Finder Agent fires

Queries Apollo.io with the research context. Returns verified decision-makers: name, title, and email where available.

4

Outreach Drafting Agent fires

Drafts one personalized cold email per contact. Grounded in the company research. Under 150 words, no generic openers, peer-to-peer tone.

✓

You receive ready-to-send emails

Copy each email with one click. Everything grounded in real data from the research brief.
